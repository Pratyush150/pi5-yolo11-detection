Pi 5 Person Detector

Real-time person detection on the Raspberry Pi 5 CPU. One small model, YOLOX-tiny, finds people in each camera frame; two video checks remove things that only look like people. No accelerator needed, and every part is free for commercial use.

How it works

From camera frame to "person"

1 cameraNewest framePi HQ camera at 1280×720. A background thread keeps only the latest frame, so output never lags. Optional 180° turn for an upside-down mount
2 modelYOLOX-tiny int8Input 416×256, the camera's 16:9 shape, so no grey padding. 29 ms per frame on 3 CPU cores
3 personPerson score ≥ 0.45Only the COCO "person" class is read. 0.45 still catches people partly hidden behind objects
4 motionHas it ever moved?A box is accepted once something moved inside it, then followed while the person keeps still. Chairs and coats never move
5 confirm3 of 5 framesA person is reported after appearing in 3 of the last 5 frames, which removes one-frame flickers

Steps 4 and 5 apply to video. For a single photo, call detect(frame, video=False).

Use it

In an application

from person_detector import PersonDetector

detector = PersonDetector()             # load once, one per camera
people = detector.detect(frame)         # frame: BGR image from OpenCV or the Pi camera
# [{"label": "person", "conf": 0.92, "box": [x1, y1, x2, y2]}, ...]

Options: PersonDetector(conf=0.45, motion=True, confirm=True, threads=3). To read the camera too, use camera.LatestFrame("auto", rotate=180). A web UI for testing (app.py) shows the live feed, the people in view and switches for each check.

Choices

Why it is built this way

DecisionReason
YOLOX-tiny, not YOLO11Apache 2.0, free to ship in a product. Ultralytics YOLO11 is AGPL-3.0: a closed product would need a paid licence
416×256 inputSame detail as 416×416 for a 16:9 camera without the grey padding. About 3× faster on the CPU
CPU, no acceleratorWorks on any Pi 5. The Hailo-8 is not needed for 20 FPS
Motion check, not a higher thresholdLook-alikes scored 0.66–0.70, as high as partly hidden real people. Movement separates them
No box mergingMerging head and legs boxes lost 65 real people in group photos and helped once. YOLOX never split a person on the camera
What to expect

Known limits

A completely still person at start-up

Delay

The motion check needs some movement. Someone already in view and motionless when the app starts is reported once they move slightly, usually within about 1.5 s.

If that matters: use motion=False, which accepts look-alikes again.

Moving cameras

Setup

On a handheld, pan-tilt or drone camera everything moves, so the motion check stops filtering.

Fix: use motion=False there and rely on the 3-of-5-frame confirmation.

Small or distant people

Accuracy

YOLOX-tiny is a small model. People under about 64 px tall in the frame are found less often.

Fix: place the camera closer, or train a larger model on your own footage.

Heat

Speed

The model keeps 3 cores busy. Without cooling the Pi 5 slows itself down at about 85 °C.

Fix: fit the Active Cooler and check vcgencmd get_throttled.
